# Ordinary differential equations: numerical methods

Lecture notes in the form of a Julia notebook (_in Spanish_):

- [Download the notebook](ODES.ipynb) · [View it online with its outputs](https://nbviewer.org/github/reula/PDE/blob/main/notes/lecture05/ODES.ipynb)

Contents: the explicit Euler method, its local and global errors and its instability for oscillatory problems; Taylor methods and the leapfrog scheme, with its spurious mode; the midpoint and Heun methods and the general two-stage Runge–Kutta family; the classical fourth-order Runge–Kutta method and embedded methods with step-size control; reduction of higher-order equations, such as the harmonic oscillator, to first-order systems, and phase portraits.
